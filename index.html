01 / About us

About Us

Partnering for Meaningful Growth for All Parties

Introduction

RM International is a second-generation business that began its journey in the early 2000s, building residential homes in Africa. As the economy shifted, we expanded our horizons by launching an office supply business in the Metro Detroit area. Since 2015, as a certified Minority Business, we’ve proudly partnered with local Detroit-based companies, including Rocket Mortgage, Piston Group, Hollywood Casino at Greektown, Gentherm, and Matrix Human Services. Our collaborations are rooted in a commitment to fostering growth for all parties involved.

Our Mission: Driving Solutions for Our Partners and Customers

At RM International, “driving solutions” is not just a phrase; it’s our operational ethos. We actively listen to our clients and respond with tailored solutions that address their unique needs. By focusing on the business-to-business market, we have streamlined our supply chain management to deliver the lowest total cost, ensuring total commitment and total satisfaction.

Our dedication to community service and support is at the forefront of our priorities. We believe that meaningful growth extends beyond business; it involves uplifting the communities we serve.
